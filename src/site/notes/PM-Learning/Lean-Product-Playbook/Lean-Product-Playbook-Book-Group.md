# Lean Product Playbook Book Group

## Overview

Book group at FamilySearch on Dan Olsen's *The Lean Product Playbook*, targeting PMs and aspiring PMs among peers and their direct reports. Follows the *Playing to Win* book group (Aug–Dec 2025), which built shared strategic vocabulary. This group shifts from strategy to craft — practical frameworks for achieving product-market fit.

## Rationale

Olsen aggregates core PM concepts — Lean Startup, Jobs to Be Done, Kano model — into a coherent **Product-Market Fit Pyramid** framework. Building this foundational knowledge helps PMs critically evaluate ideas and AI recommendations rather than accepting them at face value.

## Learning Arc

Position this as stage one of a two-stage arc:

1. **Olsen** (*The Lean Product Playbook*) — foundations and mental models
2. **Torres** (*Continuous Discovery Habits*) — ongoing discovery practice

## Format

- Weekly Friday meetings
- Chapters ≤15 pages: meet the following Friday (1 week of reading time before discussion)
- Chapters >15 pages: skip a Friday (2 weeks of reading time before discussion)
- Company holidays are skipped; next session moves to the following Friday
- Small cohort maintains energy and accountability
